Stock Prediction Dashboard

This interactive Shiny dashboard allows users to forecast stock prices using two powerful time series models: ARIMA and Prophet. Choose your stock ticker, set the date range, and visualize trends through real-time charts, candlestick plots, and technical indicators like RSI, MACD, and Bollinger Bands.

ARIMA is ideal for users who want robust forecasts based on recent time series patterns. Prophet, developed by Facebook, is more flexible for handling seasonality and missing data. Try both and compare their predictive performance!
